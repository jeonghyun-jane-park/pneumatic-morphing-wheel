# Third-party visual assets

The technical report intentionally preserves the visuals that appeared in the original project report.

- **Multi-cable morphing wheel**: attributed in the report to Sung-Hyuk Park and Dong Il Park, “Groundbreaking Morphing Wheel Technology for Enhanced Mobility,” *Lab-Worldwide*, 3 Sept. 2024. Confirm reuse permission before publishing the source image in a public repository.
- **NASA airless tire images**: attributed in the report to NASA’s Airless Tire Technology / NASA Technology Transfer Program. Review the source page and applicable NASA media guidance before public redistribution.
- **Author photographs**: prototype and deformation photographs are identified in the report as photographs by the author.
- **AI-generated concept images**: identified in the report captions as AI-generated images by the author.

If public redistribution rights for a third-party image are uncertain, keep the PDF private or replace the source image with a link/citation before making the repository public.
